# Privacy Policy — AI Visibility & GEO Audit

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This plugin is a set of instructions (skills) that runs inside Claude. It has no servers of its own.

- **No data collection.** The plugin author does not receive, store or process any data you enter or any reports Claude produces.
- **What the plugin uses.** When you run an audit, Claude may use its own web search and web fetch tools to read public web pages (for example, the website you ask to audit and its `robots.txt`). This happens inside your Claude session under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
- **Business contact details.** During an audit Claude may read publicly published business details (company name, address, phone, email) from the audited website and directories to check their consistency. These details are used only inside your conversation and are not stored or sent anywhere by the plugin.
- **Information you paste.** Answers you paste from other AI assistants stay in your Claude conversation.
- **No tracking, cookies or analytics** are added by the plugin.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/xelA31B/ai-visibility-audit/issues
