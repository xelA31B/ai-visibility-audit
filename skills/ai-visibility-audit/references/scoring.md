# Scoring rubric

Score each area 0–10. Overall AI Visibility Score = weighted sum × 10 (0–100).

| Area | Weight | 0–3 (weak) | 4–6 (average) | 7–10 (strong) |
|------|--------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| AI answer presence | 30% | Brand absent from most category/problem prompts | Mentioned for brand prompts and some category prompts | Mentioned in top positions for most category prompts |
| Sentiment & accuracy | 15% | Wrong facts, outdated info or negative tone | Mostly correct, neutral | Correct, positive, specific strengths named |
| Cited sources / off-site | 20% | Few or no listings, reviews, media | Present on main directories, some reviews | Strong reviews, media, Wikipedia/Wikidata, active community mentions |
| Site readability for AI | 15% | Content JS-only, unclear offer, no structured data | Readable, partial structured data | Clear offer, full schema.org, FAQ answering buyer questions |
| AI bot access | 10% | Search/user AI bots blocked or site unreachable | Mixed rules, no llms.txt | Search and user bots allowed, sitemap present, llms.txt present |
| Competitive position | 10% | Competitors dominate all prompts | Shares space with competitors | Leads competitors on most prompts |

## Interpreting the overall score

- **0–39** — Practically invisible to AI assistants. Fix basics first.
- **40–69** — Visible for brand queries, losing category queries to competitors.
- **70–100** — Strong. Focus on keeping information fresh and winning comparison queries.

## Rules

- Score only from evidence gathered in this session or provided by the user.
- When an area could not be checked, exclude it and re-normalize weights; state this in the report.
- Competitor scores are usually partial — label them as estimates.
